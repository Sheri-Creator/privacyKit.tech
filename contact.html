Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to get a response?

We typically respond within 24-48 hours on weekdays. For bug reports, we aim to respond within 12 hours.

Do you offer partnerships or collaborations?

Yes — we are open to collaborations with cybersecurity educators, privacy advocates, and developers. Select "Partnership / Collaboration" in the category dropdown.

Can I request a specific tool to be added?

Absolutely. Feature requests are our most valuable feedback. Select "Feature Request" and describe what tool you need and why. We prioritize based on community demand.

Is my contact information kept private?

Yes. Your name and email are used only to respond to your message. We never share, sell, or use your contact details for marketing.
