Frequently Asked Questions

Are these tools really free?

Yes — every tool on PrivacyKit is completely free to use with no hidden fees, no subscription, and no account required. We built these tools to make digital privacy accessible to everyone.

Do you store any data I enter into the tools?

No. All tools run entirely in your browser (client-side). Nothing you type — passwords, IP addresses, or emails — is ever sent to our servers or stored anywhere.

Do I need to create an account to use PrivacyKit?

No account, no login, no email required. Just open any tool and start using it instantly.

Is PrivacyKit safe to use?

Yes. Since all processing happens locally in your browser, there is nothing to intercept. Your data never leaves your device.

What tools does PrivacyKit offer?

PrivacyKit currently offers a Password Strength Checker, IP & Geolocation Lookup, and an Email Breach Checker — with more tools being added regularly.

Who is PrivacyKit designed for?

PrivacyKit is built for anyone who cares about their digital privacy — from everyday internet users to cybersecurity students, developers, and IT professionals.
